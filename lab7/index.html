What I found

The network gets busier after day 40

01

Connectivity

Daily links rise from an average of 5.5 in days 1–20 to 7.3 in days 41–60. Day 45 is the busiest frame, with 10 links and all 12 companies active.

02

Activity shifts

Fusion Electronics leads the first 40 days. In the last 20, Ion Systems and Cedar Retail become the two largest companies by transaction volume.

03

Clusters

The daily network is never one connected component. Early low-activity days such as 1, 7, 13, and 19 split into three separate pairs.

04

Recurring ties

Several pairs reappear on a six-day cycle. Delta Components–Fusion Electronics is the largest recurring relationship, totaling about $440,000 across 20 active days.

05

Regional mixing

Cross-region links increase from 56% in the first 20 days to 73% in the last 20. The later network is both denser and less regionally separated.

Design note

Reading the animation

The layout keeps the same company objects and only restarts the force simulation gently when the links change. Region-based forces also pull companies toward stable areas of the chart. This does not freeze the graph, but it avoids a complete rearrangement from one day to the next. Fading links show arrivals and departures; the slider is better for comparing specific dates than relying on animation alone.

Analysis

I used 1,200 records from the Twitter US Airline Sentiment dataset. After parsing the dates and retweet counts, I removed 155 duplicate tweet IDs, standardized usernames and whitespace, and selected 200 tweets per airline. I kept the original wording for sentiment analysis because punctuation and negation can affect meaning. Sentiment was estimated with the Twitter RoBERTa model, and the score is the positive probability minus the negative probability. The chart shows the percentage of negative, neutral, and positive tweets for each airline. US Airways and American have the largest negative shares, at 64.0% and 62.5%. Virgin America has the largest positive share at 38.5% and is the only airline with a positive average score. Delta is more evenly divided across the three categories. These values are model estimates rather than ground-truth judgments, so sarcasm and ambiguous wording may still be classified incorrectly.
